\end{abstract}

\section{The FDM Tension and the Chameleon EFT}

We resolve this by formulating FDM as an environment-dependent scalar field. Following the Damour-Polyakov conformal expansion, the effective mass shifts dynamically according to the local baryonic density $\rho_b$:
\begin{equation}

\end{equation}

In the baryon-dense Milky Way disk, the coupling dominates, elevating the mass to $m_{\text{eff}} \gtrsim 10^{-21}$ eV. This reduces the de Broglie wavelength locally, preserving stellar streams.
