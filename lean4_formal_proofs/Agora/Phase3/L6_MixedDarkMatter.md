# The $L_6$ Picard-Fuchs Operator and Mixed Dark Matter

$$L_6 = L_4 \times L_2$$

2. **The $L_2$ Block**: An apparent singularity block. This block sources a much heavier, classical particle component.

This mathematical factorization strongly predicts a **Mixed Dark Matter (MDM)** universe. Instead of assuming the ultra-light axion comprises 100% of the dark matter, the geometry dictates that the dark sector is a composite: a fraction $f_{FDM}$ is the ultra-light quantum scalar field (from $L_4$), and the remaining $(1 - f_{FDM})$ is a standard, classical Cold Dark Matter (CDM) field (from $L_2$).

## Astrophysical Consequences

Dwarf galaxies are dominated by the inner core structure. The $L_4$ ultra-light axion, even at a fraction $f_{FDM} \sim 10-20\%$, condenses into a macroscopic ground-state soliton. This soliton establishes a central density core, effectively smoothing out the divergent CDM cusp generated by the $L_2$ component. Thus, the successful resolution of the core-cusp problem is preserved.

### 2. The Stellar Stream Rescue
In a 100% FDM universe, the $1.71 \times 10^{-23}$ eV axion produces quantum interference granules of massive scale ($M_g \sim 10^{10} M_\odot$), which violently disrupt cold stellar streams in the Milky Way (e.g., the GD-1 stream).

However, in the MDM model, the granules are exclusively formed by the coherent interference of the FDM fraction.
- The local FDM density drops to $\rho_{FDM} = f_{FDM} \cdot \rho_{local}$.
- The mass of an individual granule drops proportionally: $M_g \approx \rho_{FDM} \lambda_{dB}^3 \propto f_{FDM}$.
- The dynamical heating rate on stellar streams scales as $d\sigma^2/dt \propto \rho_{FDM} \cdot M_g$.

Combining these factors, the heating rate is **quadratically suppressed**:
$$ \frac{d\sigma^2}{dt} \propto f_{FDM}^2 $$

If the $L_4$ axion fraction is naturally restricted by the $L_6$ geometry to roughly $10\%$, the dynamical heating rate drops by a factor of 100. The massive granules become "ghostly," lacking the mass and density to disrupt the GD-1 stream, thereby saving the $S_{20}$ geometric model from observational falsification.
