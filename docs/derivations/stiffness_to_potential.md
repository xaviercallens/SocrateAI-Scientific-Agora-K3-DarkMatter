# GAP-2 Physics Derivation Memo: From Recurrence Data to Potential Curvature (Task T2.2)

**Question this memo answers:** does the algebraic quantity `V''(0) = 1014` (S₁,₂) /
`336` (S₂,₁), traced end-to-end in `docs/derivations/stiffness_pipeline.md` (T2.1),
actually *equal* the curvature of the physical axion potential that sets the axion
mass — as its name and its use in `lean4_formal_proofs/Agora/GaugeCoupling.lean` and
`Agora/Phenomenology/PTAFrequencyRatio.lean` assume?

**Short answer:** the mechanical step (mirror-map coefficients → Taylor curvature of
a cosine-sum potential) is a genuine, checkable derivation. But tracing where the
repository's *actual* cited axion masses (`3.18×10⁻²¹` eV, `1.83×10⁻²¹` eV —
`CAVEATS.md §1`, `PARAMETER_LEDGER.yaml`) come from in the live code
(`scripts/k3_sieve_analysis.py`) uncovers a further problem, not previously
documented: **those masses are not actually generated by "the stiffness" `1014`/`336`
at all.** At the τ values the code uses to produce them, the instanton sum is
single-term-dominated to ~90 decimal places, so `q₂`, `q₃` (and hence 1014 vs 336)
are numerically irrelevant to the masses that were reported. The near-agreement
between √(1014/336) ≈ 1.7372 and the actual mass ratio (≈1.7378, computed below) is
explained entirely by the free per-vacuum choice of τ, not by the topology. This
directly affects how `PTAFrequencyRatio.lean` (T2.3) should be described.

## Part 1 — What IS derived: mirror-map coefficients → potential curvature

Given the standard Svrcek–Witten-style instanton-sum axion potential (already used
for S₂₀ in `scripts/extract_axion_potential.py`):

$$V(x) = \Lambda^4 \sum_{d=1}^{D} q_d\bigl(1-\cos(dx)\bigr), \qquad x = \phi/f_a$$

this is a genuine, mechanical Taylor-expansion fact — no physics assumed yet:

$$V''(0) = \Lambda^4 \sum_{d=1}^{D} q_d\, d^2$$

because $\frac{d^2}{dx^2}[1-\cos(dx)]\big|_{x=0} = d^2$ exactly, for every $d$. This
step is **DERIVED**, independent of what $q_d$ or $\Lambda$ physically mean. It is
what `docs/derivations/stiffness_pipeline.md` Step 4 computes (with $\Lambda^4=1$,
$D=3$), giving 1014 and 336.

## Part 2 — What is ASSUMED: $q_d$ = physical instanton weights

The mirror-map coefficients $q_d$ (Step 2 of the T2.1 pipeline) are pure arithmetic
objects: coefficients of the canonical coordinate $q(z)=z\exp(\tilde y(z)/y_0(z))$
built from the Picard–Fuchs solution of $S_{A,B}(n)$. Nothing in their construction
refers to a compactification, a brane, or an instanton. Re-interpreting them as the
Gromov–Witten / instanton-counting weights that appear in a genuine Euclidean
D3-brane superpotential,

$$W \sim \sum_d q_d\, e^{-2\pi d \tau}, \qquad m_a^2 \sim \frac{M_{\rm Pl}^2}{\mathcal V}\sum_d d^2 q_d\, e^{-2\pi d\tau}$$

(the Svrcek–Witten (2006) formula, reproduced in `scripts/mass_from_first_principles.py`
and `scripts/k3_sieve_analysis.py`) is a **physics ansatz, not a derivation from this
model's K3 geometry**. It is the standard heuristic for CY/K3 instanton superpotentials
in the string literature, but connecting it *specifically* to the $S_{1,2}$/$S_{2,1}$
algebraic family requires the top-down compactification data (OPEN_PROBLEMS.md items
1–2: concrete orientifold, integer flux quanta, explicit brane wrapping a definite
cycle) that this repository does not have and will not fabricate. This matches T2.1's
own flag on this step and is not re-litigated further here — it is simply named as
**Assumption A1** below.

## Part 3 — A further problem: "stiffness" and "instanton sum" are not the same object

Even granting Assumption A1, there is a second, independent gap that T2.1 did not
surface because it only traced the *arithmetic* pipeline (`k3_sieve_analysis.py:191`,
`V_double_prime = sum(q_int[d] * d**2 for d in range(1,4))`) — with **no**
$e^{-2\pi d\tau}$ suppression at all. But the same script, a few lines later
(`k3_sieve_analysis.py:196-201`), computes the *actual physical mass* using a
**different** quantity that *does* include the suppression:

```python
tau = 33.6255 if A == 1 else 33.8014
inst_sum = sum((d**2) * q_int[d] * np.exp(-2 * np.pi * d * tau) for d in range(1, 4))
m_a = (2.4e27 / np.sqrt(1e4)) * np.sqrt(max(0, inst_sum))
```

Running this exact code (verified independently below, not merely read) reproduces
the cited masses exactly: $m_a(S_{1,2})=3.180\times10^{-21}$ eV,
$m_a(S_{2,1})=1.830\times10^{-21}$ eV, matching `CAVEATS.md §1` and
`PARAMETER_LEDGER.yaml` to 3 significant figures.

**The problem:** at $\tau\approx33.6$–$33.8$, $e^{-2\pi\tau}\approx1.8\times10^{-92}$.
Since $q_1=1$ for *both* sequences (T2.1's own table), the $d=1$ term of `inst_sum`
is $\approx e^{-2\pi\tau}$, while the $d=2$ term is smaller by a further factor of
$q_2\cdot4\cdot e^{-2\pi\tau}\approx10^{-90}$, and the $d=3$ term smaller still. In
other words: **`inst_sum` — the quantity that actually determines the reported axion
masses — is dominated by the $d=1$ term to roughly 90 decimal digits.** The integers
1014 and 336 get essentially all of their value from $d=2,3$ ($981/1014\approx97\%$
and $315/336\approx94\%$, per T2.1's table) — precisely the terms that are
astronomically irrelevant once the physical exponential suppression is switched on.

Concretely, to extremely high precision:

$$\frac{m_a(S_{1,2})}{m_a(S_{2,1})} \;\approx\; \sqrt{\frac{q_1 e^{-2\pi\tau_{12}}}{q_1 e^{-2\pi\tau_{21}}}} \;=\; e^{-\pi(\tau_{12}-\tau_{21})} \;=\; e^{\pi\cdot0.1759} \;=\; 1.7378\ldots$$

— a number that depends on $\tau_{12}-\tau_{21}$ alone, **not on $q_2$, $q_3$, and
hence not on 1014 vs. 336 at all.** Independently evaluating the code's own
`inst_sum` (not the approximation above) gives $m_a(S_{1,2})/m_a(S_{2,1})=1.73778$,
confirming the approximation is accurate to 5 significant figures. Compare to the
claimed topological ratio $\sqrt{1014/336}=1.73720$: the two agree to $\sim0.03\%$,
but **for a reason that has nothing to do with the stiffness integers** — it is a
property of the specific, hardcoded $(\tau_{12},\tau_{21})=(33.6255, 33.8014)$ pair,
which is not derived anywhere in this codebase from the K3 geometry.

### Why this looks like circularity, not coincidence

`scripts/mass_from_first_principles.py` is a *separate, later* script whose own file
header states, verbatim:

> "The circular fitting against pre-assumed targets (target_mass_S12 = 3.18e-21,
> target_mass_S21 = 1.83e-21) has been REMOVED. Those targets originated from
> spin-down phenomenology; using them to back-calibrate (tau, V) and then claiming
> the model 'predicts' the masses is logically circular."

That script now honestly reports only an *achievability sweep* over free $(\tau,\mathcal
V)$ — no unique values (consistent with `CAVEATS.md §1`'s existing caveat). But
`scripts/k3_sieve_analysis.py` — the script that actually *produces* the numbers
currently cited in `CAVEATS.md`, `K3_DISCOVERY_REPORT.md`, and
`PARAMETER_LEDGER.yaml` — still hardcodes exactly two $\tau$ values, one per vacuum,
with **no derivation, citation, or fitting procedure documented anywhere**, that
reproduce those exact same pre-assumed target masses to 3 significant figures. No
commit message, docstring, or comment in `k3_sieve_analysis.py` explains how
33.6255 and 33.8014 were obtained. The most parsimonious explanation consistent
with the evidence is that these two numbers were back-solved from the target masses
using the same practice `mass_from_first_principles.py` explicitly disavows — but
this cannot be confirmed or refuted from the repository as it stands; **it is
flagged here as an open provenance question, not asserted as proven fact.**

## Consequence for the T2.3 / PTAFrequencyRatio.lean falsification test

`Agora/Phenomenology/PTAFrequencyRatio.lean`'s docstring currently states the ratio
test is a prediction "independent of every uncertain modelling choice upstream"
because $\tau,\mathcal V$ "cancel exactly in the ratio." **This is only true if the
mass ratio genuinely equals $\sqrt{V''(0)_{12}/V''(0)_{21}}=\sqrt{1014/336}$ as a
matter of the model's physics.** Part 3 above shows that, in the model's own
currently-used numbers, the mass ratio is instead governed almost entirely by the
*difference* of two hardcoded, undocumented $\tau$ values — a free modulus pair that,
per `CAVEATS.md §1` itself, is "not uniquely fixed by the K3 topology." The
τ-cancellation argument in the Lean docstring is **not what actually happens in this
model's own mass pipeline**: nothing currently derives $\tau_{12}-\tau_{21}=0.1759$
from geometry, so nothing currently *guarantees* the ratio stays near
$\sqrt{1014/336}$ under a legitimate future re-derivation of $\tau$.

**What remains true and kernel-verified, unaffected by this finding:** the pure
arithmetic statement $1014/336 \in (1.73^2, 1.75^2)$
(`GaugeCoupling.lean:inv_coupling_ratio_in_interval`) is exact rational arithmetic
and stays correct regardless of any of the above. What is **not** established is the
physical claim that this ratio equals the true axion-mass ratio, independent of free
moduli — the central premise the PTA falsification test relies on for its
"parameter-free" framing.
