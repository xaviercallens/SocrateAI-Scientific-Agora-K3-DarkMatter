# Lee & Tsai (2026) — Naturally resonant dark matter from extra dimensions

**Citation:** T. Lee¹ and Y.-D. Tsai²³⁴, *Naturally resonant dark matter from extra dimensions*, Phys. Rev. D **114**, L011701 (2026). DOI: 10.1103/tsq1-bhsz. Received 12 Apr 2025; accepted 22 Apr 2026; published 8 Jul 2026.
¹Indiana University Bloomington · ²University of Manchester · ³LANL · ⁴University of Sheffield.

**Summary (5 lines):** Explores the mass-resonance structure that naturally arises in extra-dimensional models, demonstrated with **fermionic dark matter and a dark photon** on an S¹/(Z₂×Z₂′) orbifold. The KK tower produces resonances that can enhance both DM **annihilation** and **self-interactions**. The construction embeds a DM **axial-vector coupling** to the dark photon, opening otherwise-excluded parameter space. Presents unique predictions for **direct-detection experiments and accelerator searches**. It is a particle-physics resonance model — not an ultralight/axion model.

**Epistemic classification:** Mainstream theoretical particle cosmology (peer-reviewed PRD Letter).

**Role in v2:** Conceptual bridge for the KK-resonance structure only. **The particle content differs from ours in every respect that matters for detection** (fermion vs. axion; GeV-scale-ish direct-detection/accelerator phenomenology vs. 10⁻²¹ eV wave dark matter; annihilation resonance vs. instanton mass generation).
