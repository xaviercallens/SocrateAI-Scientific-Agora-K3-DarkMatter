# El Naschie (2013) — A Resolution of Cosmic Dark Energy via a Quantum Entanglement Relativity Theory

**Citation:** M. S. El Naschie, *A Resolution of Cosmic Dark Energy via a Quantum Entanglement Relativity Theory*, Journal of Quantum Information Science **3**, 23–26 (2013). DOI: 10.4236/jqis.2013.31006.

**Summary (5 lines):** Derives Ω_Λ ≈ 0.72 (and Ω_matter ≈ 0.045) from transfinite E-infinity set-theoretic arguments built on golden-ratio combinatorics, presented as a resolution of the dark-energy problem via "quantum entanglement relativity." No dynamical field equations, no Lagrangian, no falsifiable observational program distinct from the numbers it reproduces. Published in a venue outside the mainstream cosmology literature. The numerical coincidences are not derived from any mechanism that could have failed to produce them.

**Epistemic classification:** **Numerology-class** — the framework is widely regarded in the mainstream literature as non-dynamical numerology. This classification is the reason it is archived here.
