| Sequence | m_a (eV) | σ₈ suppression |
|---|---|---|
| S12 | 3.180e-21 | 0.0000% |
| S21 | 1.830e-21 | 0.0000% |

**Finding: negligible suppression (0.0000% for S21, the lighter mass).** This model's axion masses ($\sim1.8$–$3.2\times10^{-21}$ eV) are $\sim20$–$30\times$ heavier than the canonical FDM "sweet spot" ($\sim10^{-22}$ eV) where quantum-pressure suppression matters at the $R=8\,h^{-1}$Mpc scale relevant to $S_8$. **The standard FDM mechanism the model's own narrative invokes does not produce an observable $S_8$ shift at these masses.**

## 2. Growth-factor channel (the epsilon-modified background from T5.2)

Treating $\epsilon$ as a $c_s^2=0$ Generalized Dark Matter background modification (as literally specified for the CLASS treatment), the linear growth factor ratio is $D_\epsilon(a{=}1)/D_{\rm std}(a{=}1) = 1.0403$, implying an $S_8$ shift of **+4.03%** — the WRONG SIGN (an increase, not the decrease needed to help the S8 tension). This is because the anomalous CDM dilution *enhances* early matter density (see T5.2's finding that recombination-era CDM density is boosted), which speeds up structure growth rather than suppressing it.
