        Source: GDZ Goettingen, which hosts Crelle vol. 341 OPENLY —
          https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/download/pdf/PPN243919689_0341/LOG_0016.pdf
          (plain unauthenticated curl; the De Gruyter/Brill page IS paywalled and EuDML's

        SCAN, no usable text layer (1686 chars extracted). Verified by RENDERING the pages
        and reading them directly; page images from pdftoppm, PDF page = article page - 191.
        Title page (p.193), verbatim: "The purpose of this paper is to study the rational
          Hodge structure attached to the second rational cohomology group H^2(Y,Q) of a
          SMOOTH IRREDUCIBLE PROJECTIVE SURFACE Y over the field C of complex numbers. We
          shall prove that the Hodge group of Y acts IRREDUCIBLY on the Q-lattice V(Y) of
          transcendental cycles if the geometric genus P_g is one (par. 1)."
        p.200, definitions: A(Y) = H^2(Y) cap H^{1,1}(Y) is "the lattice of algebraic
          cycles"; "Let us denote the rank of A(Y) by rho(Y). rho(Y) is called the Picard
          number of Y." Then "Let V(Y) be the ORTHOGONAL COMPLEMENT of A(Y) in H^2(Y,Q)",
          with H^2(Y,Q) = A(Y)_Q (+) V(Y) and **dim_Q V(Y) = b_2(Y) - rho(Y)**.
          => V(Y) IS the transcendental lattice tensored with Q.
        p.207, Theorem 1.6, verbatim: "Let P_g = h^{2,0}(Y) = 1 (for example Y is a K3
          surface). Then: a) The Hdg-module V(Y) is SIMPLE and the algebra E = E_Y is a
          commutative field; b) U(Y) subset mu_E subset E* where mu_E is the group of roots
          of unity in E."
        HYPOTHESES: smooth irreducible projective surface over C, p_g = 1. NO genericity
          hypothesis. Holds for every projective K3.
        NOTE: the proof 1.6.1 opens "By (1.5.1) the Hdg-module V(Y) is simple", but the item
          printed as 1.5.1 is the Albert-classification statement about E — the internal
          cross-reference looks like a misprint. The STATEMENT of 1.6 a) is unambiguous and
          is what this repo cites.
